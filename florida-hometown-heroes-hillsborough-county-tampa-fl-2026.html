What the Florida Hometown Heroes Program Actually Is

Florida Hometown Heroes is a state-funded down payment assistance program administered by the Florida Housing Finance Corporation. It was created to help essential workers purchase homes in the state where they work — people who serve their communities but often can't afford to live in them.

The assistance is a deferred second mortgage at 0% interest. You don't make monthly payments on it. The balance is only repaid when you sell, refinance, transfer ownership, move out, or pay off your first mortgage. It sits quietly in the background until you're done with the home.

The DPA amount is calculated as 5% of your first mortgage, with a minimum of $10,000 and a maximum of $35,000:

  • $450,000 purchase with a conventional loan: approximately $22,500 in assistance
  • $550,000 purchase: approximately $27,500
  • $700,000 purchase or higher: the maximum $35,000

The assistance covers your down payment, closing costs, or both — in whatever combination gets you to the table.

Who Qualifies in Hillsborough County

There are two categories of requirements — occupation and financial. You need to meet both.

Occupation eligibility

Only one borrower on the loan needs to work in a qualifying field. The eligible categories in 2026 include:

  • Healthcare: nurses, physicians, nurse practitioners, therapists, pharmacists, medical technicians, dental professionals, and other licensed healthcare workers
  • Education: K-12 teachers, administrators, school counselors, aides, librarians, and bus drivers; college faculty and staff also qualify
  • First responders: law enforcement officers, firefighters, EMTs and paramedics, 911 dispatchers, corrections officers, probation officers
  • Military and veterans: active-duty U.S. military, National Guard and Reserve members, and honorably discharged veterans — veterans must also be employed full-time by a Florida-based employer
  • Court system and childcare: state and local court employees; licensed childcare workers

One requirement that catches people off guard: you must work for a Florida-based employer and report to a physical Florida location. Fully remote employees are not eligible. If you're hybrid, you must report on-site at least three days per week.

Financial requirements for Hillsborough County

The 2026 income limit for Hillsborough County — part of the Tampa-St. Petersburg-Clearwater MSA, which includes Hillsborough, Pinellas, and Pasco — is $156,450. This is set at 150% of the area median income and was updated in January 2026. All borrowers on the loan count toward the income figure.

The other financial requirements:

  • 640 minimum credit score for all borrowers
  • First-time homebuyer status — no ownership of a primary residence in the past three years. Veterans and buyers in federally designated targeted areas are exempt.
  • The property must be your primary residence, and you must occupy it within 60 days of closing
  • Complete a HUD-approved homebuyer education course before closing (VA borrowers are exempt)

What Loan Types Work with Hometown Heroes

The program pairs with standard mortgage products, each with different loan limits and down payment requirements:

  • FHA: 3.5% minimum down, 640+ credit score. The 2026 FHA loan limit in Hillsborough County is $541,287. Most common for buyers with credit scores in the 640–700 range.
  • Conventional: 3–5% minimum down, 640+ credit score. The 2026 conforming loan limit is $832,750, well above most Tampa purchases in the $400K–$700K range.
  • VA: No down payment required, no loan ceiling, 640+ credit score. Veterans who qualify for VA financing can direct the full $35,000 DPA toward closing costs — potentially closing with minimal cash out of pocket.
  • USDA: No down payment, 640+ credit score, property must be in a USDA-eligible area. In Hillsborough County, this covers outer areas including parts of Riverview, Wimauma, and Plant City.

The Funding Reality: Why Timing Is Everything

The Florida Legislature appropriated $50 million for Hometown Heroes in the 2025–2026 budget cycle. That sounds substantial. In context, it isn't.

In prior program years, the $100 million allocation was exhausted in under 30 days. With half the funding this cycle, the runway is shorter. When the money runs out, the program pauses — sometimes for months — until the Legislature appropriates more. Buyers who were in the process but hadn't locked their DPA reservation when the funds ran dry lose their place in line entirely.

The practical takeaway: if you're eligible, start the process now. Your lender reserves DPA funds only once you're under contract — but getting pre-approved, completing your homebuyer education certificate, and identifying participating lenders takes time. Don't be in the position of completing those steps the week the allocation runs out.

How to Apply, Step by Step

The process runs through a participating lender. You cannot apply directly through Florida Housing.

  1. Verify your eligibility. Confirm your occupation qualifies, check that your household income is under $156,450 for Hillsborough County, and verify your first-time buyer status (or veteran exemption).
  2. Find a participating lender. Search the authorized lender list at floridahousing.org. Not all lenders participate in the program, so you need one specifically approved for Hometown Heroes.
  3. Get pre-approved. Your lender verifies income, occupation eligibility, and credit. Pre-approval typically takes 1–2 business days.
  4. Complete a HUD-approved homebuyer education course. These are online, take 6–8 hours, and cost $75–$99. The certificate is valid for two years. VA borrowers are exempt.
  5. Get under contract on a home. Once you have a signed purchase agreement, your lender reserves your DPA funds in Florida Housing's system on a first-come, first-served basis. This is the step that actually secures your money.
  6. Close. The assistance — up to $35,000 — is applied at closing toward your down payment and closing costs.

One additional note: the program charges no application fee. If anyone is asking for upfront payment to access Hometown Heroes, that is a scam.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the income limit for the Florida Hometown Heroes program in Hillsborough County in 2026?

The 2026 income limit for the Tampa MSA — which covers Hillsborough, Pinellas, and Pasco counties — is $156,450. This is 150% of the area median income, updated January 2026. Income is calculated across all borrowers on the loan, not just the borrower in the qualifying occupation. If your combined household income exceeds this figure, you're ineligible regardless of your occupation.

Who qualifies for the Florida Hometown Heroes program?

Full-time employees of Florida-based employers in healthcare, education, first responder fields, military and veteran occupations, court system roles, and childcare. Only one borrower needs a qualifying job. Remote-only workers don't qualify; hybrid workers must be on-site at least three days per week. Your employer's HR documentation confirms your eligibility — it's verified, not self-reported.

How much down payment assistance does Florida Hometown Heroes provide in 2026?

Up to $35,000, calculated as 5% of your first mortgage amount with a $10,000 minimum. On a $500,000 purchase, that's approximately $25,000. On a $700,000 purchase or higher, you reach the $35,000 cap. The assistance is a 0% interest deferred second mortgage — no monthly payments, repaid when you sell, refinance, or move out.

Do I have to be a first-time homebuyer to use the Hometown Heroes program in Tampa?

Generally yes — no ownership of a primary residence in the past three years. But veterans with an honorable discharge are exempt from this requirement, as are buyers purchasing in federally designated targeted areas. If you're a veteran who sold a home two years ago and now want to buy in Tampa, you may still qualify. Ask a participating lender to confirm your specific situation.

Can the Florida Hometown Heroes funds run out?

Yes, and this has happened in prior years. The 2025–2026 allocation is $50 million, down from $100 million in prior years, and funds are distributed first-come, first-served. Your DPA is reserved only when you're under contract and your lender submits through Florida Housing's system. Getting pre-approved before you find a home is the only way to be ready to move the moment you're under contract.
