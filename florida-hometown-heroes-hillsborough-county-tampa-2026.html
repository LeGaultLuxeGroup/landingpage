Florida Hometown Heroes Program in Tampa, FL: Hillsborough County Income Limits, Down Payment Assistance, and How to Apply in 2026

What is the Florida Hometown Heroes Program and how does it work for Tampa buyers?

The Florida Hometown Heroes Program provides up to $35,000 in down payment and closing cost assistance to eligible first-time homebuyers working in qualifying professions in Hillsborough County. The assistance is a 0% interest, deferred second mortgage with no monthly payments — it's due only when you sell, refinance, or move out. For 2026, the Hillsborough County income limit is approximately $104,160 for 1–2 person households and $121,520 for households of three or more. Eligible professions include teachers, nurses, firefighters, law enforcement, EMTs, military personnel, childcare workers, and 50+ additional categories.

By Amanda LeGault, LeGault Luxe Group at Keller Williams | November 17, 2026

If you work in an essential profession and you're trying to buy your first home in Tampa, there's a program specifically designed for you — and most people who qualify haven't heard of it.

The Florida Hometown Heroes Housing Program gives eligible buyers up to $35,000 toward their down payment and closing costs. That's not a grant you have to compete for in a lottery. That's a structured second mortgage at 0% interest, with no monthly payment, available through Florida Housing Finance Corporation to buyers who qualify by profession and income.

In a Tampa market where the median home price hovers around $420,000 and buyers in Riverview, Brandon, and New Tampa are competing against investors and relocating professionals, $35,000 in down payment assistance is the difference between getting in now and waiting another two years.

Here's exactly how the program works, what the 2026 Hillsborough County limits are, and what you need to do to use it.

Who Qualifies — Professions and Residency

The Florida Hometown Heroes Program was designed for the workforce that keeps communities running. As of the July 2026 update, the program covers 50+ eligible profession categories. The core eligible groups include:

  • K–12 teachers, instructional staff, and school administrators
  • Nurses, nurse practitioners, physician assistants, and allied health professionals
  • Law enforcement officers (municipal, county, and state)
  • Firefighters and paramedics
  • EMTs and emergency medical services personnel
  • Childcare workers and early education professionals
  • Active military and veterans
  • Corrections officers
  • Social workers and behavioral health counselors
  • Transportation and infrastructure workers in state-eligible roles

Beyond profession, you must meet three other conditions: you must be a first-time homebuyer (or not have owned a primary residence in the past three years), you must plan to occupy the home as your primary residence, and you must work full-time in your eligible profession — part-time employment does not qualify.

2026 Hillsborough County Income Limits

This is where a lot of potential buyers get tripped up. The Hometown Heroes income limits are set at 150% of the area median income (AMI) for each county, updated annually by HUD. For Hillsborough County in 2026:

Household Size2026 Income Limit (Approx.)
1–2 persons$104,160
3+ persons$121,520

These figures are based on the 2026 Hillsborough County AMI figures from Florida Housing Finance Corporation. Your lender will pull the official limits at the time of your application — the numbers above are the current 2026 guidance, but always confirm with your approved HHH lender before making financial decisions based on them.

The income limit applies to all borrowers on the loan. If you're buying with a co-borrower, both incomes count toward the limit. For many dual-income households in Tampa, this is an important calculation — a nurse earning $78,000 married to a teacher earning $62,000 would have combined income of $140,000, which exceeds the household limit for Hillsborough County. The program is targeted at individual earners and moderate-income households, not high-earning dual incomes.

How the Down Payment Assistance Actually Works

The $35,000 isn't a grant. It's a second mortgage, structured like this:

  • 0% interest — no interest accrues
  • No monthly payments — you're not adding to your monthly debt load
  • Deferred — the balance is due when you sell, refinance, transfer title, or stop occupying the home as your primary residence
  • Not forgivable — when you eventually sell or refinance, you repay the original $35,000 amount

In practice, this means you're getting a no-interest loan for your down payment. If you buy a $420,000 home, use a conventional loan, and put down 3.5% ($14,700), the Hometown Heroes $35,000 covers your entire down payment plus a substantial portion of your closing costs. You could close on a home in Riverview or Brandon with significantly less out of pocket than you'd need without the program.

The purchase price limits for Hillsborough County in 2026 are set at the FHA loan limit for the area — currently approximately $524,250 for a single-family home with an FHA loan and around $726,200 for conventional financing. This covers a wide range of the first-time buyer market in Riverview, Brandon, Temple Terrace, and New Tampa, though it generally excludes luxury homes in South Tampa and Davis Islands.

The Steps to Apply

The process is more structured than a typical mortgage, but it's manageable. Here's the sequence:

  1. Find a Florida Housing-approved lender. Not every lender participates in the Hometown Heroes program. You need a lender certified by Florida Housing Finance Corporation. I can connect you with approved Tampa-area lenders who have done dozens of these transactions — they know how to structure the offer and work with listing agents who've never seen a Hometown Heroes deal before.
  2. Get pre-approved for both the first and second mortgage. Your lender will run your HHH pre-approval alongside your primary loan approval. This gives you a full picture of what you can afford and what your true out-of-pocket costs will be.
  3. Start your home search with your budget confirmed. With $35,000 accounted for, you'll know exactly what price range makes sense. Many buyers in Riverview and Brandon find they can target homes $50,000–$75,000 higher than they thought because their cash requirement is so much lower.
  4. Make an offer — structured correctly. This is where having an experienced agent matters. Hometown Heroes offers require specific addenda, disclosure language, and sometimes seller cooperation with the second mortgage terms. A listing agent unfamiliar with the program can kick back the offer unnecessarily. I've written and negotiated Hometown Heroes offers in Hillsborough County and can make sure yours reads like any other clean offer.
  5. Close and file your homestead exemption. After closing, remember: the homestead exemption deadline in Florida is March 1 of the year following your purchase. If you close before December 31, 2026, you can file and save significantly on your 2027 property tax bill with the Hillsborough County Property Appraiser.

Using Hometown Heroes for New Construction in Tampa

Riverview and Brandon are ground zero for new construction in Hillsborough County right now — communities like South Fork, Panther Trace, Triple Creek, and Summerfield have active builder inventories and buyers are absolutely using Hometown Heroes to get in.

The key caveat: you need to use a Florida Housing-approved lender, not the builder's in-house lender. Most production builders (D.R. Horton, Lennar, Pulte, etc.) push their own mortgage companies hard and will offer rate buydowns or closing cost credits to keep you in-house. Those offers aren't bad — but you can't layer Hometown Heroes on top of a non-approved lender. You'll need to compare the total picture: builder incentives through their lender vs. Hometown Heroes through an approved lender.

In many scenarios, $35,000 in Hometown Heroes assistance outweighs a $10,000–$15,000 builder closing credit. Run both scenarios before you commit to either path. This is exactly the kind of side-by-side analysis I walk clients through before we go to contract.

How much down payment assistance does the Florida Hometown Heroes program provide?

Florida Hometown Heroes provides up to $35,000 in down payment and closing cost assistance as a 0% interest, non-amortizing second mortgage with no monthly payments. The loan is deferred and due upon sale, refinance, or transfer of the property, or when the home is no longer your primary residence.
