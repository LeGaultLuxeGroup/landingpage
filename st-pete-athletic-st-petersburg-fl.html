St. Pete Athletic: Where Sports, Social Life, and Community Come Together

By Amanda LeGault, Licensed FL REALTOR® (#SL3506757) · LeGault Luxe Group · Posted October 2026

St. Pete Athletic is a paddle sports and social club at 680 28th St. S. in St. Petersburg's Warehouse Arts District, right off the Pinellas Trail. It has 14 indoor pickleball courts plus padel and table tennis, and Rose's Dining & Drinks on site. In September 2026, CourtSource ranked it the No. 1 pickleball club in the U.S.

St. Petersburg, Florida, has no shortage of places to explore, but some destinations offer more than just another place to spend an afternoon. They're places where people can stay active, meet new people, enjoy a great meal, and become part of the community.

St. Pete Athletic brings all of that together under one roof.

Located in St. Petersburg's Warehouse Arts District, St. Pete Athletic is a paddle sports and social club that combines indoor athletics, dining, and social experiences in roughly 50,000 square feet at 680 28th St. S., right off the Pinellas Trail (St. Pete Catalyst, January 2026). Competitive players, people trying a new sport and anyone looking for a place to meet friends all have a reason to visit, and the club offers a different take on the traditional athletic facility.

More Than a Place to Play

At first glance, St. Pete Athletic might look like a destination for sports enthusiasts. But the concept goes beyond the courts.

The club brings together three paddle sports: pickleball, padel, and table tennis. With indoor courts and dedicated playing areas, visitors can enjoy everything from casual games to organized leagues, tournaments, and coaching sessions.

The variety makes it appealing to people with different interests and experience levels. Beginners can learn the fundamentals, while more experienced players can work on their technique and compete with others.

And because the facility is designed around both athletics and socializing, the experience doesn't have to end when the game does.

Pickleball, Padel, and Table Tennis Under One Roof

One of St. Pete Athletic's defining features is its combination of three paddle sports in a single location.

Pickleball continues to attract players looking for a fun, accessible way to stay active and connect with others. The club features 14 indoor pickleball courts, providing space for open play, skill development, leagues, and tournaments. In September 2026, the national platform CourtSource ranked it the No. 1 pickleball club in the U.S. (St. Pete Catalyst, Sept. 18, 2026).

Padel offers another option for racquet-sport enthusiasts. Played on an enclosed court with glass walls, the sport combines elements of tennis and squash, creating a fast-paced game that rewards teamwork and strategy.

Table tennis rounds out the experience, giving visitors another way to enjoy friendly competition in a social setting.

For anyone interested in trying something new, having these options in one location makes it easier to explore different sports and discover what they enjoy.

Where Athletics Meets Hospitality

What makes St. Pete Athletic particularly interesting is the way it combines recreation with hospitality.

The facility includes Rose's Dining & Drinks, where guests can enjoy meals and beverages alongside their athletic activities. There's also a coffee bar, making the venue a place to visit beyond scheduled games or workouts.

That combination creates flexibility. You might spend the morning on the pickleball courts, meet friends for lunch, or stop by to enjoy the atmosphere without playing a sport at all.

The club also hosts social events and private gatherings, extending its role beyond athletics.

For people who appreciate having several ways to spend their time in one destination, that combination is part of the appeal.

A Different Kind of Community Space

A good community gathering place gives people a reason to return.

At St. Pete Athletic, that opportunity comes through shared activities. Players can join leagues, attend clinics, participate in tournaments, or simply meet others who enjoy the same sports.

The club's programming also welcomes people at different stages of their athletic journey. You don't have to be an experienced player to get started, and you don't need to make competitive sports your entire lifestyle to enjoy the facility.

Sometimes, the appeal is simply having somewhere to go, something to do, and an opportunity to connect with people outside your usual routine.

That sense of connection is an important part of what makes local destinations worth discovering.

What St. Pete Athletic Adds to the St. Petersburg Lifestyle

When people consider moving to a new city, the home itself is only part of the decision.

They also want to know what everyday life might look like. Where will they spend their weekends? What activities will be available nearby? Where can they meet friends, pursue their interests, and feel connected to the community?

Destinations like St. Pete Athletic offer one example of the recreational and social experiences available in St. Petersburg.

Its location in the Warehouse Arts District also places it within a part of the city known for its creative character and evolving mix of businesses and cultural spaces.

For prospective residents, exploring places like this can provide a better sense of the lifestyle a neighborhood offers beyond its homes and real estate listings.

Of course, the right fit depends on your priorities. Some people value proximity to parks and waterfront trails, while others prioritize restaurants, arts and entertainment, fitness facilities, or social opportunities. Discover More of St. Petersburg

St. Petersburg offers a variety of ways to stay active, explore local businesses, and connect with the community. St. Pete Athletic is one destination worth knowing about, whether you're already a local or considering making the area your home.

If you're interested in exploring the club, you can learn more about its sports, programming, dining, and membership options on the official St. Pete Athletic website. Full membership was listed at $250 a month as of December 2025 (I Love the Burg), and the club capped memberships at about 800 by January 2026, so confirm current pricing and waitlist status directly with the club.
