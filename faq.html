Buying on the Gulf Coast

Yes — relocation is one of Amanda's specialties. She works frequently with remote and hybrid professionals moving to the Gulf Coast from major metros like Chicago, New York, and the Bay Area.

Amanda helps relocation buyers navigate neighborhood comparisons, waterfront tradeoffs, Florida's unique insurance and tax landscape, HOA considerations, and how to avoid overpaying in a market that looks simple but has real hidden complexity.
For most buyer transactions, Amanda's representation is covered through the seller's side of the commission — meaning no out-of-pocket cost to you as the buyer. Since the NAR settlement changes in 2024, buyer agency agreements are now standard. Amanda will walk you through how compensation works before you sign anything, with full transparency. Schedule a no-obligation consultation to get started.
Florida's insurance landscape is one of the most important — and most misunderstood — parts of buying on the Gulf Coast. Amanda helps buyers understand:

• Flood zone designations (AE, X, VE) and how they affect insurance costs
• The difference between homeowners insurance and flood insurance
• How elevation certificates can reduce premiums
• Which neighborhoods and building types carry the most insurance risk

This is a conversation Amanda has with every buyer before making an offer — it's too important to leave until after closing.

Download Amanda's free Seller Playbook for the full breakdown.
Pricing is one of the most consequential decisions in a sale. Amanda uses a combination of comparable sales analysis, active competition review, absorption rate data, and current buyer demand signals — not just a Zestimate.

She looks at what has actually closed in the last 60–90 days in your specific neighborhood, adjusts for condition and features, and factors in days-on-market trends to price your home to sell quickly and at maximum value. Request a home value analysis to get started.

Investing & Short-Term Rentals

Yes. Amanda works with investors on short-term rental properties, long-term investment properties, development parcels, and commercial real estate along the Gulf Coast.

She provides market-specific rental income analysis, DSCR loan scenario modeling, HOA and zoning due diligence (critical for STR investments), and access to off-market opportunities. If you're evaluating a specific property, Amanda can help you stress-test the numbers before you commit.
STR viability depends heavily on local zoning, HOA rules, and proximity to demand drivers. Generally speaking, barrier island communities (Siesta Key, Anna Maria, Treasure Island) and beach-adjacent neighborhoods in St. Pete and Sarasota have historically strong rental demand — but each has different regulations.

Amanda conducts thorough STR due diligence for every investment client, including checking city/county ordinances, HOA bylaws, and projected occupancy rates based on comparable rentals. Talk to Amanda about specific markets you're considering.

Gulf Coast Market

The Gulf Coast market — particularly St. Petersburg, Sarasota, and Tampa Bay — continues to attract significant buyer interest from across the country. Key dynamics Amanda watches:

• Inventory has risen from pandemic lows, giving buyers more negotiating leverage than in 2021–2022
• Luxury and waterfront properties remain in strong demand, especially from relocation buyers
• Insurance costs are reshaping affordability in certain flood-prone areas
• Interest rates continue to influence buyer behavior and days-on-market

For a current analysis of your specific neighborhood or price range, contact Amanda directly.
